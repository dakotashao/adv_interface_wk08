# adv_interface_wk08

Advanced Interface, week 8: Menu and Toolbar.

| Set | Concept | Prototype |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One-Line Plan**: a plan written as one sentence, *I want to [code] for [45 min], then [take a walk] for [10 min], with [rain] →*. Each colored word drops its own options. Time words grow with their minutes; square corners mark the choice; sound starts with categories: nature and scene each open a row of their sounds to the right, white noise and silence are picked directly. ← → fine-tunes time, numbers pick, Enter starts; the finished half of the sentence folds away as the plan runs | [Open](https://dakotashao.github.io/adv_interface_wk08/one-line-plan/) |
| 2 | **Margin Toolbar**: a reading toolbar for iPad and Apple Pencil, in a book read together with friends. Four tools (Highlight, Pen, Comment, Eraser) and the options of the tool in hand, always on show; a white sheet glides to each choice. Drag the bar to any edge and it docks there, horizontal or vertical, at any alignment; leave it in the middle and it floats. Drag its end to shrink it from icon + text to icons, then into a ring. Push it on into its edge and only the options stay, flush like a drawer. The Pencil switches tools: hold, slide, let go. Comments are handwritten in the margin and signed with each reader’s sticker; pages turn like a book | [Open](https://dakotashao.github.io/adv_interface_wk08/margin-toolbar/) |
| 3 | **Workout Watch**: a workout menu on the watch. Choosing a workout enters its session and the screen becomes its colour; everything is set on one page (Effort, Goal, Mode, Start) with groups that open in place. Forward is left → right: rows fill from the left and leave to the right, the chosen row's colour runs on and opens into the screen. The item you are on rides to the middle of the screen | [Open](https://dakotashao.github.io/adv_interface_wk08/workout-watch/) |
| 4 | **Workout Game**: a living-room console workout picker on a dark tile floor. Every selection uses the screen transition's colours: cards light up by Sweep or Ripple, rows by an arrow that drags the colours in (→ turns ↓ to open, ↗ to start). The figure previews each choice: it runs faster on Hard, and the opponent you hover runs beside you | [Open](https://dakotashao.github.io/adv_interface_wk08/workout-game/) |
| 5 | **Radical Swap**: a character study for learners, on iPad. The right side is a practice sheet: pinyin in four lines over a tian-zi grid (tap it to hear the word), and the stroke order built up box by box underneath. Point at a part and the left column explains it (火 fire, carries the meaning, also in 炒 灯 烧); tap again to reach a single stroke, its name and direction. The menu is set into the column under what you are pointing at: listen, write it, lift it out, and swap a part to make a new character (秋 → 和 → 利), with a trail to go back | [Open](https://dakotashao.github.io/adv_interface_wk08/radical-swap/) |

Each set lives in its own folder with a self-contained index.html. Spec is inside each prototype (Spec button).
