# adv_interface_wk08

Advanced Interface, week 8: Menu.

| Set | Concept | Prototype |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One-Line Plan**: a plan written as one sentence, *I want to [read] for [45 min], then [take a walk] for [10 min], with [rain] →*. Each colored word drops its own options. Time words grow with their minutes; square corners mark the choice; sound starts with categories: nature and scene each open a row of their sounds to the right, white noise and silence are picked directly. ← → fine-tunes time, numbers pick, Enter starts; the finished half of the sentence folds away as the plan runs | [Open](https://dakotashao.github.io/adv_interface_wk08/one-line-plan/) |

Each set lives in its own folder with a self-contained index.html.
