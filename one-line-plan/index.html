Tap a colored word, or press A F B T S. Enter starts.

Rules

  • One sentence is the menu. White words are fixed; each colored word is a trigger that drops its own list.
  • Length is time. Both time words share one scale: width grows from 130 px to a fixed 260 px cap at 60 min, so 30 min sits halfway. Time moves in 5-minute steps.
  • Square corners mean selected. Hover squares an option as a preview; the open trigger flattens its bottom so the list hangs from it.
  • Color is category. Each word has its own color; in sound, blue is nature, green is scene, lilac picks directly.
  • Solid and line alternate. Icons inside one list switch between a solid shape and a thin line, like the reference.
